Themed fire

Every theme has its own costumes and music. These are our signature ones. For large events we create a new theme to brief.

Rock

Raw, loud, rebellious. Fire to a driving rock energy.

Fantasy

Ethereal and mythic. A fairy-tale told in flame.

Post-apocalyptic

Gritty, industrial, dystopian. Fire from a scorched future.

Steampunk

Plague doctors and Victorian dames, in brass, gears and gaslight.

Slavic Folk

Pagan motifs and maidens in white. A folkloric fire ritual drawn from old Slavic myth.
