  • Dragon Fire Show — A fire theatrical show built around a 3-metre dragon, performed in a classic fantasy or a Slavic folk theme.
  • Fire Show — Spectacular fire props (wings, cubes, sparkle staffs, flamethrowers and fire cannons) in a visual theme of your choice, from fantasy to post-apocalyptic and rock.
  • LED Fire Show — The best of both: live fire performed in glowing LED costumes. The heat of fire with the colour of light.
  • LED Show — Pure light, zero smoke or flame: glowing LED costumes and programmable props create a stunning visual show for indoor venues.
