LED Show

A dazzling, smoke-free light show of glowing LED costumes and eye-catching props, from light-up wings to glowing cubes. We can brand the props with your logo, or scale it up into a full club party. Perfect for indoor venues where open flame is not an option.

  • Duration: 10–15 min · adaptable
  • Format: Indoor & outdoor
  • Cast: Scalable
